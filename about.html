About The Proximity Club

BuildingMeaningfulRelationships.

A Houston community where professionals, entrepreneurs, and leaders come together to create opportunity through authentic connection.

Why We Exist

Success is rarely achieved alone.

The most meaningful opportunities, lasting partnerships, and transformative ideas are born through authentic relationships.

The Proximity Club was founded to create a place where professionals, entrepreneurs, business leaders, and community changemakers could connect with purpose, collaborate with intention, and grow together.

Today, we’re building a community where relationships come before transactions and collaboration creates opportunity.

Our Mission

Building meaningful relationships that move business and community forward.

Everything we do is guided by this mission. Through intentional networking experiences, educational programming, mentorship, community service initiatives, and strategic partnerships, we create opportunities that empower our members to grow personally, professionally, and collectively.

What We Believe

Our values guide every connection we create.

Authentic Relationships

We believe genuine connections create stronger businesses, stronger leaders, and stronger communities.

Collaboration Over Competition

Our members understand that shared success creates greater impact than individual achievement alone.

Professional Excellence

We encourage continuous learning, leadership development, and a commitment to excellence in every endeavor.

Community Impact

We measure our success by the positive difference we make in the communities we serve.

Diversity of Thought

Different perspectives drive innovation and create stronger outcomes together.
